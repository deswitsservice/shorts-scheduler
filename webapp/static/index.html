Install the free helper

Shorts Everywhere posts from your own computer, with your own signed-in Chrome. Nothing about your videos or logins goes through this website. You install the helper once (Mac, with Google Chrome).

  1. Open Terminal (press ⌘ + Space, type Terminal, press Return).
  2. Paste this and press Return: 
    curl -fsSL https://minefoundation.org/install.sh | bash
  3. If Chrome asks to let this site access other apps and services on this device (or your local network), choose Allow.

Using Safari? It can’t reach the helper from this page; open 127.0.0.1:8765 after installing.
